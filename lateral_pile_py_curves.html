Static P-y Curve Generator

Matlock (1970) soft clay and API RP 2A sand p-y curves for laterally loaded pile analysis.

Comma-separated list of depths below ground surface.
Typically 0.005 to 0.02 for soft clays.
Typically 0.5 for soft Gulf of Mexico clays.
Also expressed in kN/m² / m. Varies with density.
