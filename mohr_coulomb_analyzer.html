Mohr-Coulomb Failure Envelope Analyzer

Determine the effective cohesion (c') and friction angle (φ') from triaxial test stages by linear regression of σ1 on σ3, and plot the Mohr circles with the failure envelope.

Enter the effective principal stresses at failure for each test stage (σ3 = confining stress, σ1 = major principal stress), all in the same stress unit. At least two stages with different confining stresses are required.

Formulation

Failure envelope: \( \tau = c' + \sigma' \tan\phi' \).
Regression on the stages: \( \sigma_1 = m\,\sigma_3 + b \), with \( m = \tan^2(45^\circ + \phi'/2) \) and \( b = 2c'\tan(45^\circ + \phi'/2) \).
Hence \( \sin\phi' = \dfrac{m-1}{m+1} \) and \( c' = \dfrac{b}{2\sqrt{m}} \) (negative values are clamped to zero).
