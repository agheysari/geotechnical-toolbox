Fundamental Weight-Volume Equations

Fundamental Phase Identity: $$S_r \cdot e = w \cdot G_s$$

Unit Weight Relations: $$\gamma_d = \frac{G_s \cdot \gamma_w}{1 + e} = \frac{\gamma}{1 + w}, \quad \gamma = \frac{(G_s + S_r \cdot e) \cdot \gamma_w}{1 + e}, \quad \gamma_{sat} = \frac{(G_s + e) \cdot \gamma_w}{1 + e}$$

Void Ratio and Porosity: $$e = \frac{n}{1 - n}, \quad n = \frac{e}{1 + e}$$
