Cooper-Jacob Approximation:
Valid for small \( u = \frac{r^2 S}{4 T t} \) (typically \( u < 0.01 \)).

\( T = \frac{2.303 Q}{4 \pi \Delta s} \)
\( S = \frac{2.25 T t_0}{r^2} \)

Where \( \Delta s \) is drawdown per log cycle and \( t_0 \) is the time intercept.
