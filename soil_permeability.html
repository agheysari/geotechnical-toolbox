Governing Equations & Reference

Constant Head Method (ASTM D2434):
Used for permeable coarse-grained soils ($k > 10^{-3}\text{ cm/s}$): $$k = \frac{V \cdot L}{A \cdot h \cdot t}$$

Falling Head Method (ASTM D5084):
Used for fine-grained silts and clays ($k < 10^{-3}\text{ cm/s}$): $$k = \frac{a \cdot L}{A \cdot t} \ln\left(\frac{h_1}{h_2}\right) = 2.303 \frac{a \cdot L}{A \cdot t} \log_{10}\left(\frac{h_1}{h_2}\right)$$

Hazen's Empirical Formula (Clean Sands):
Approximation based on effective grain size $D_{10}$ in mm ($C \approx 1.0$): $$k = C \cdot (D_{10})^2 \quad [\text{cm/s}]$$
