Atterberg Limits & Flow Curve Plotter

Plot the liquid limit flow curve (moisture content vs. number of blows on a semi-log scale) to interpolate the Liquid Limit, Flow Index, Plasticity Index and Liquidity Index.

Enter the moisture content at each blow count from the liquid limit test (ASTM D4318). The flow curve is a least-squares line of moisture content against log10(blows); the liquid limit is its value at 25 blows.

Formulation

Flow curve: \( w = -I_f \log_{10}(N) + C \)  (least squares on \( \log_{10} N \)).
\( LL = w \) at \( N = 25 \), \( PI = LL - PL \), \( LI = \dfrac{w_n - PL}{PI} \).
