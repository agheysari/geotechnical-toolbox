USCS Soil Classifier

ASTM D2487 coarse and fine-grained decision tree with Casagrande plasticity chart classification and group symbols.

Enter the soil properties below to classify according to the Unified Soil Classification System (ASTM D2487).

1. Soil composition (must sum to 100%)
2. Gradation parameters (coarse soils)
3. Atterberg limits (fine and plastic soils)
4. Modifiers
5. Missing-data fallbacks

Optional assumptions applied only when the corresponding laboratory data is unavailable.
