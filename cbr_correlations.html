Input Guidelines

The AASHTO 1993 correlation \( M_R = 1500 \times CBR \) is generally valid only for fine-grained soils with a CBR below about 10%.

For higher CBR values the MEPDG relationship \( M_R = 2555 \times CBR^{0.64} \) is often more appropriate.

Step-by-step calculation

    Formulation

    DCP to CBR (USACE, Webster et al. 1992): \( CBR = 292 / DPI^{1.12} \), with DPI in mm/blow.
    Resilient modulus, AASHTO 1993: \( M_R\,(\text{psi}) = 1500 \times CBR \) (Heukelom & Klomp, 1962).
    Resilient modulus, MEPDG (NCHRP 1-37A): \( M_R\,(\text{psi}) = 2555 \times CBR^{0.64} \).
    Modulus of subgrade reaction, AASHTO 1993: \( k\,(\text{pci}) = M_R\,(\text{psi}) / 19.4 \). Conversions: 1 psi = 0.00689476 MPa, 1 pci = 0.271447 MPa/m.
