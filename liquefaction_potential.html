Idriss & Boulanger (2008) Governing Equations

Cyclic Stress Ratio (CSR): $$CSR = 0.65 \cdot \left(\frac{PGA}{g}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{\sigma_{vo}}{\sigma'_{vo}}\right) \cdot r_d$$ $$\text{where } r_d = \exp\left(\alpha(z) + \beta(z) \cdot M_w\right)$$

Overburden & Fines Corrected SPT Blow Count: $$(N_1)_{60} = N_m \cdot C_N \cdot \left(\frac{ER}{60}\right) \cdot C_B \cdot C_R \cdot C_S, \quad (N_1)_{60,cs} = (N_1)_{60} + \Delta (N_1)_{60}$$

Factor of Safety (FS): $$FS_{liq} = \left(\frac{CRR_{7.5}}{CSR}\right) \cdot MSF \cdot K_\sigma$$
