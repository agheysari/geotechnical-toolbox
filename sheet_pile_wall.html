Method summary

Pressures (Rankine, smooth wall): \( p_a = K_a\sigma'_v - 2c'\sqrt{K_a} \ge 0 \), \( p_p = (K_p\sigma'_{v,f} + 2c'\sqrt{K_p})/F_p \), plus water pressure on each side.
Cantilever (simplified method): \( \sum M_O = \int_0^{H+D_0} p_{net}(H + D_0 - z)\,dz = 0 \); the counter-pressure below the point of rotation O is replaced by a force R, developed by the extra embedment.
Anchored, free earth support: \( \sum M_A = \int_0^{H+D_0} p_{net}(z - a)\,dz = 0 \), \( T = \int_0^{H+D_0} p_{net}\,dz \). Shortest pile, largest moment.
Anchored, fixed earth support (Blum): zero moment at the point of contraflexure \( z_c \), taken at zero net pressure; \( T = \frac{1}{z_c - a}\int_0^{z_c} p_{net}(z_c - z)\,dz \), then \( R_c x + \int_{z_c}^{z_c+x} p_{net}(z_c + x - z)\,dz = 0 \). Longer pile, smaller moment.
Water: hydrostatic pressures carry any difference in water level to the toe. Steady seepage loses the head uniformly along the flow path round the toe, which also lowers the passive effective stress in front; neither model is always conservative.
\( D = (1 + \text{increase})\,D_0 \), \( Z_{req} = M_{max}/\sigma_{all} \).
